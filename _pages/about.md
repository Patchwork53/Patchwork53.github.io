I'm a CS PhD student at the University of California, Riverside, advised by [Yue Dong](https://yuedong.us/). I study how multimodal and long-context models actually use what they see and read — and when that behavior becomes unsafe.

My current work spans **multimodal interpretability and safety**, **long-context safety**, and **AI for science**. That includes figuring out when vision-language models skip visual detail in favor of language shortcuts, how models fail to refuse harmful goals they infer over long contexts, and how generative models can be used for problems like antibody design and RNA modeling.

I spent the summers of 2025 and 2026 as an Applied Scientist intern at Amazon in San Diego, working on buyer-risk prevention. Before UCR, I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology.
